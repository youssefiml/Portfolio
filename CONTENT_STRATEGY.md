# Content strategy

## Positioning and audience

**Youssef Imlilss — Full-Stack Software Engineer**. Lead with frontend engineering, product development and UI/UX; support the full-stack claim with specific, attributed project evidence rather than an undifferentiated technology list. Primary audience: hiring managers, engineering leads and product teams assessing hands-on engineering and collaboration. Confirm target roles, location/remote preferences and whether freelance clients are also a priority.

Suggested introductory direction, subject to approval: “I build product interfaces and full-stack applications, with a focus on clear user experiences and practical engineering.” Avoid “expert,” years of experience, seniority, ownership and outcome claims until confirmed. The requested headline is a positioning choice, not verification of a formal job title.

## Proposed information architecture

1. **Introduction:** name, role, concise value proposition; primary link to selected work and secondary contact link.
2. **Selected work:** 2–3 evidence-backed projects with problem, personal role, technologies, contribution and a visual; link to fuller case studies and public source where appropriate.
3. **How I work / capabilities:** frontend, backend and product/UI practice described through demonstrated work; distinguish AI and mobile exposure from proven ownership and depth.
4. **About:** confirmed background, professional context, collaboration style and relevant experience; optional concise résumé only if current and approved.
5. **Contact:** verified contact methods and availability. Avoid stale age or a frequently outdated experience count.
6. **Case study pages or accessible detailed sections:** direct links, scannable headings, a narrative and supporting screenshots. Choose routing and implementation only after scope and content are agreed.

## Case-study order

1. **BOARDING — flagship frontend/product case.** Show the registration flow and reusable checkbox/radio controls linked to the authored commit; explain actual personal decisions, validation and constraints once confirmed. The README's wider product architecture is context, not a list of individually owned achievements.
2. **DigiAssistant — full-stack/AI candidate.** Explore the diagnostic interface, API and AI integration; distinguish implemented features from README promises and explain Youssef's verified role. Confirm proprietary-use permissions and redact all sensitive data.
3. **HKids — product and systems candidate.** Show child reader and content administration, emphasizing the documented POC and device constraints. Confirm actual contribution and obtain approved visuals. WALLFIT is an alternative where its team context and personal role can be accurately credited.
4. **Additional work:** concise links to StudyGuide, Happynation and other projects only when their descriptions, ownership and availability are confirmed. Keep YSK private unless the owner explicitly approves an independently shareable description and assets.

## Case-study template

- Project name, one-sentence purpose, project status and timeframe.
- Team and explicit personal role; credit other contributors where appropriate.
- Problem and target user, with source for any claims.
- Constraints and key decisions; 2–3 concrete contributions supported by commit, file or approved artifact.
- Product flow and architecture, using annotated approved screenshots or a diagram only where useful.
- Result and evidence: actual measured outcome with source and period, or a factual delivered capability; use “not measured” when appropriate.
- Lessons, tradeoffs and next steps if substantiated. Public repository/demo links only after access and permissions are checked.

## Evidence and assets to collect

For each shortlisted project: confirm dates, collaborators and exact ownership; explain the challenge, decision, technical tradeoffs, project stage, user feedback and independently verifiable outcomes; identify approved code/commit links; supply permission to name the project/client and reuse visuals. Obtain 2–4 clean screenshots per case: overview, a key interaction, an important state such as validation or empty state, and a mobile view where relevant. Capture realistic but fictional data, remove personal/client details, and verify copyright. DigiAssistant has a public image asset that still needs review. Obtain an approved portrait only if desired, a current résumé if offered, a social preview and canonical domain.

## Questions to resolve before implementation

- Which target roles, markets and languages should the portfolio prioritize? Is the requested title a current role or aspirational positioning?
- What are the verified dates and years of experience? Which technologies can Youssef personally explain and demonstrate, including any testing, DevOps, AI and mobile work?
- What did Youssef personally design, implement and ship on each shortlisted project? Who else contributed? Which project outcomes were measured and how?
- Are BOARDING, DigiAssistant, HKids and WALLFIT public-facing, current, and approved for naming, screenshots and links? What can be said publicly about YSK, if anything?
- Which live demos, repository URLs, contact links, domain and résumé are accurate and safe to share? Are any screenshots, testimonials or client data restricted?
- Does the portfolio need a printable résumé/PDF? What deployment target and accessibility/performance requirements apply?

Do not implement until these documents have been reviewed and the evidence needed for selected work is available. Avoid invented metrics, implied sole ownership, claims of production readiness, and unsupported proficiency levels.
