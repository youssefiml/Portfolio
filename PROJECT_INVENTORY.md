Dependency presence alone is not proof of use or proficiency.

## Attribution and review policy

Use “contributed to” or describe a specific authored change where the commit supports it. Do not infer individual ownership of a repository-wide architecture from account ownership. Confirm names and roles of collaborators, date, problem, decisions, constraints, actual results and permission for all case studies. Cite code links where they illuminate a claim; do not expose credentials, personal information, client data or private implementation details. Commit evidence is a useful starting point, not proof of the entire history.
